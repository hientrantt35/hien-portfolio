HELLO, MY NAME IS

Hien Tran

UX DESIGNER

Living in Japan, I was continuously amazed by how everyday products reflected such thoughtful care for customers. These moments sparked my admiration for thoughtful design and, eventually, my interest in UX.

Working at the intersection of designers, engineers, and clients across cultures, and being close to the design process, has taught me not only to design with greater empathy for users, but also to understand and collaborate with the people who bring those experiences to life.

BEFORE DESIGN

I studied Japanese and later worked in Japan, where my experience at a staffing agency taught me to listen closely to people's needs, including what they don't always say. I then moved into IT, where I became curious about how technology shapes everyday experiences.

INTO DESIGN

As a project coordinator at a design company, first in Japan, then after moving back to my home country, I worked at the intersection of designers, engineers, and clients across cultures. Being close to the design process made me want to move from coordinating design to making design decisions myself, that became the turning point toward UX.
